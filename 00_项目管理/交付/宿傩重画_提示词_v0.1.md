## 一、这次 v2 差在哪（对照五条悟 v2）

| # | 问题 | 怎么看出来的 | 根子在提示词哪里 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **皮肤脏、纹身糊成一片**：胸口、肩膀、脸上一堆棕黑笔画，缩到 64 格就变成泥点 | 战斗图逐格看；常态每张图 5.8 万色，五条悟 4.3 万 | 写了 `tattoos on the forehead, cheeks, shoulders and upper body`，没限定条数和线宽，GPT 就画满 |
| 2 | **额头印记画成一个圆圈加点，像第三只眼** | 战斗图每格都有 | 没说印记形状 |
| 3 | **头发发灰、发土**，侧面和背面底层是棕色，像两种发色 | 行走图左、右、上三行最明显 | `dusty rose-pink` 本身就是灰粉；`darker burgundy underside` 没给颜色值，GPT 落到棕色 |
| 4 | **两种形态不是同一个人**：完全体头发是亮粉（饱和度 0.76），常态是灰粉（0.55）；裤子阴影一个偏紫一个偏灰 | 量了两套头发像素 | 常态和完全体各写一套外观，也没共用调色 |
| 5 | **描边弱，轮廓发软** | 外缘像素只有 29% 是深色描边，五条悟是 43% | 写了 `thin selective dark outlines, limited dark rim`，GPT 理解成「少描」 |
| 6 | **贴边、裁切**：逃跑两帧手碰到格子右边（x=63），倒地帧左右都贴边（x=1～64） | 外接框量出来的 | 只写了 `generous gutters`，没给硬性边距 |
| 7 | **完全体四只手看不清**：下面那对手贴着胯，和身体糊在一起，很多帧数不出四只手；身形被五条悟带瘦了 | 52 帧逐张看 | 让五条悟当「站姿参考」，GPT 把五条悟的瘦身形也搬过来了；下臂没要求和身体分开 |
| 8 | 色阶多、细碎 | 常态每帧约 1600 色 | 没说「每种材质最多 3 阶、不要抖动、不要纹理」 |

合格的地方：尺寸、格子、帧序都对；站姿可见高 58，和五条悟 59 一致；脚底贴格底；表情动作基本跟旧图走。

## 三、提示词（直接贴）

每次 = 【共用段】＋【反向约束】＋那一步的【尾段】。完全体再插入【完全体段】。

### 【共用段】（每次都贴）

```
Transparent RGBA pixel-art game sprite artwork.
Every sprite will be reduced to a 64x64 battle cell or a 48x48 map cell, so design for that final size:
large flat color clusters, at most 3 tones per material plus one outline tone, no dithering, no texture, no noise, no tiny specks.

Rendering style (take it from the style reference only): crisp high-end 32-bit JRPG chibi sprite, about 2 heads tall,
light from the upper left, one restrained cool rim light on the right edge, selective outlines:
every silhouette edge pixel is an outline pixel, and the outline is a darker shade of the local color
(never pure black on skin or hair).

Character appearance (keep identical in every sprite):
a broad-shouldered muscular young man, bare-chested.
Hair: short spiky hair swept up and back, bright salmon pink — light #F4A7B5, mid #D8728A, shadow #9C4560,
with a short dark burgundy undercut at the sides and back of the head #5E2438. Never brown, never grey, never dusty.
Eyes: narrow and sharp, red #C0283A.
Skin: warm peach — light #F6CDB0, mid #E0A283, shadow #B4735E, outline #5A2E28.
Trousers: ivory-white, loose and wide — light #F2F0EA, mid #C9C6D3, shadow #8F8AA3, outline #4A465C.
Sash: wide black sash at the waist #1C1820 with one highlight tone #3A3442. Shoes: simple dark sandals.

Body markings — exactly these and nothing else, each one a single crisp near-black (#1A1214) line,
one pixel wide at final size:
a small two-pronged mark (like a short fork, two short vertical strokes joined at the bottom) in the center of the forehead;
two short parallel horizontal stripes on each cheek just under the eye;
two parallel horizontal bands across the chest;
one band around each upper arm and one around each wrist.
Skin between the markings is clean flat tones.
```

### 【反向约束】（每次都贴）

```
Do NOT:
- add any markings beyond the list above, or paint hatching, smudges, scribbles or dirt on the skin;
- draw the forehead mark as a circle, an eye or a symbol with a dot;
- make the hair dusty, greyish, brown or two different colors, or change hair, skin or trouser colors between sprites;
- copy the hair, face, clothes or slim body of the style reference;
- let any hand, foot, hair spike or lying body touch or cross its cell edge — keep at least 3 px of clear space on every side at final size;
- merge two sprites, leave a cell empty, add extra sprites or change the cell order;
- use a checkerboard, white or colored background; everything outside the character is fully transparent, including gaps between limbs;
- add ground shadow, aura, glow, effects, weapons, text, labels, frames or grid lines.
```

### 第 0 步　常态定妆

```
IMAGE 1: the current battle wait sprite of this character — pose, facing (left) and placement reference.
IMAGE 2: the approved style reference sprite — rendering style and chibi proportions ONLY.
Draw ONE single sprite: the battle wait pose facing screen left, fists raised in a loose guard, weight on the back foot,
moderate three-quarter turn (the far eye shows as a thin sliver).
Exactly two arms. Full body, centered, generous transparent padding. 1024x1024.
```

### 第 1 步　常态行走图

```
IMAGE 1: the current map walk sheet — authoritative for pose, orientation, frame order and baseline of each cell.
IMAGE 2: the approved style reference walk sheet — rendering style ONLY.
IMAGE 3: the approved master sprite of this character — identity and palette.
Exactly two arms in every sprite.
Exactly 12 distinct sprites in precisely 3 columns and 4 rows with equal regularly spaced cells, portrait 3:4.
Row 1 faces forward/down: left step, standing, right step.
Row 2 faces viewer's left: walk step, standing, walk step.
Row 3 faces viewer's right: opposite walk step, standing, walk step.
Row 4 faces away/up: left step, standing, right step — no face at all; the back shows the burgundy undercut and the pink spikes, and the arm bands.
Obvious alternating feet, unchanged baseline in every row. Same character size in every cell. 1024x1536.
```

### 第 2～4 步　常态战斗（3 张）

共用尾段：

```
IMAGE 1: the current battle sheet section — authoritative for each cell's pose, facing, hand position, baseline and frame order.
IMAGE 2: the approved style reference battle sheet — rendering style ONLY.
IMAGE 3: the approved master sprite of this character — identity and palette.
Exactly two arms in every sprite.
Exact sprite-sheet layout: 18 separate sprites, 6 columns, 3 rows, evenly spaced, landscape about 1.84:1.
Each row holds two successive three-frame actions. Keep every sprite facing screen left unless the reference faces right.
Same character size in every cell; crouched and lying poses keep their height relative to standing poses.
1536x1024.
```

再按张接一句：

| 步 | 接这句 |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | `Row 1: walk three frames, then wait three frames. Row 2: chant three frames, then guard three frames. Row 3: damage recoil three frames, then evade three frames.` |
| 3 | `Row 1: thrust three frames, then swing three frames. Row 2: missile three frames, then skill three frames. Row 3: spell three frames, then item three frames. Make the leading hand clearly different from frame to frame.` |
| 4 | `Row 1: escape three frames (facing exactly as in the reference, hands kept inside the cell), then victory three frames. Row 2: dying (low-HP hunch) three frames, then abnormal status three frames. Row 3: sleep crouch three frames, then dead three frames. The three dead sprites lie sideways, low at the bottom of their cells, the whole body including the feet inside the cell with clear space on both ends.` |

### 【完全体段】（第 5～8 步插在共用段后面）

```
This is the four-armed form of the SAME man: identical head, hair, palette, trousers, sash and markings as the master sprite.
Additional features of this form only:
a second, smaller pair of eyes directly under the main eyes, drawn as short red slits;
a second pair of arms attached at the lower ribs, under the upper arms.
Four-arm rules:
- the upper pair performs the action exactly as in the stance reference;
- the lower pair stays bent with closed fists at hip height, one hand slightly in front of the hips and one slightly behind,
  both always visible; each lower arm is separated from the torso by its outline, and is one tone darker than the upper arms
  so it reads as further back;
- four hands are countable in every sprite, except where the reference hides a hand behind the body;
- the torso is as broad as the normal form's, not slimmer.
Exactly four arms in every sprite.
```

### 第 5 步　完全体定妆

```
IMAGE 1: the approved normal-form master sprite — identity, palette, stance, body width and camera angle.
IMAGE 2: the current four-armed wait sprite — only where the lower arms go.
Draw ONE single sprite: the four-armed form in the same battle wait pose and the same three-quarter turn as IMAGE 1,
facing screen left. Full body, centered, generous transparent padding. 1024x1024.
```

### 第 6～8 步　完全体战斗（3 张）

```
IMAGE 1: the approved four-armed master sprite — identity and palette.
IMAGE 2: the approved NORMAL-form battle sheet of the same section — authoritative for each cell's stance, turn, body width,
upper-arm action, baseline and frame order.
IMAGE 3: the current four-armed battle sheet of the same section — only for where the lower arms go and for frame order;
never copy its proportions or colors.
Exact sprite-sheet layout: 18 separate sprites, 6 columns, 3 rows, evenly spaced, landscape about 1.84:1.
Each row holds two successive three-frame actions. 1536x1024.
```

再接第 2～4 步同一行的那句。第 6 步多一句：`The wait action only needs its first frame; draw the other two wait cells identical to the first.`（游戏里完全体待机只用 1 帧。）第 8 步多一句：`Victory: one upper fist raised high, the other three hands at clearly different heights.`

## 四、后处理（出图后到游戏格）

在方案 4.3 流水线上按宿傩改：

| 步 | 做什么 | 宿傩的数 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 检查透明：全不透明按角色抠底；棋盘格就重出 | |
| 2 | 切格：等分；一格两个人、或人跨格就重出 | 行走 3×4；战斗 6×3 |
| 3 | 定缩放：每张母图用**同一个比例**，按待机（行走按正面站立）那格算 | 战斗站姿可见高 **58**；行走正面站立高 **45**。完全体和常态用同一个比例 |

| 5 | 对齐：每格按**旧图同一格的可见底边**对齐，水平按旧图可见中线 | 完全体对齐旧完全体逐帧图 |
| 6 | alpha 二值化：≥128 保留，其余全透明 | |
| 7 | **吸附调色板**：和第三节色值距离 ≤24 的像素直接改成那个色值，其他不动 | 这一步专治问题 3、4：两种形态、所有帧的头发和皮肤颜色统一 |

## 五、验收清单（Kyo 看图时对一遍）

| 项 | 合格 |
| --- | --- |
| 纹身 | 只有额头叉形、两颊各两道、胸前两道、上臂和手腕各一圈；皮肤其他地方干净 |
| 额头 | 不是圆圈、不像眼睛 |
| 头发 | 亮鲑鱼粉，侧后是深酒红短发，不发灰、不发棕；两种形态一样 |
| 描边 | 轮廓每一圈都有深色描边，皮肤和头发的描边带本色 |
| 边距 | 逃跑、倒地帧手脚不碰格边 |
| 四只手 | 完全体每帧数得出四只手，下面一对在胯部前后，不贴身体 |
| 身形 | 完全体和常态一样宽，不比常态瘦 |
| 大小 | 站姿可见高约 58，和五条悟 v2 并排一样高 |
